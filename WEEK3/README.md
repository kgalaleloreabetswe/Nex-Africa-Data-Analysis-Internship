
# Week 3 — Product & Customer Analysis

## Overview

Week 3 focused on analysing product and customer performance using the Superstore dataset to identify top-performing products, loss-making areas, customer purchasing patterns, and opportunities for business improvement.

The analysis was performed using PostgreSQL and Excel, focusing on product sales, profitability, sub-category performance, customer purchasing behaviour, and customer segmentation.

## Objectives

- Identify the top 10 products by sales and profit
- Identify the bottom 10 products by profit
- Analyse sales, profit, quantity, and profit margins across product sub-categories
- Identify loss-making products and sub-categories
- Determine the most profitable and most popular sub-categories
- Analyse customer sales, profitability, and purchasing frequency
- Calculate orders per customer and average customer order value
- Segment customers according to their purchasing value and profitability
- Identify the top 10 customers by number of orders
- Develop actionable business insights and recommendations

## Key Outcome

The Superstore dataset was analysed to evaluate product profitability, category performance, and customer purchasing behaviour.

The analysis identified the Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier as the leading product by both sales and profit, Technology as the best-performing category, and Tables as a loss-making sub-category requiring further attention. Customer purchasing behaviour and profitability were also examined to support customer segmentation and identify opportunities for improved business performance.

The findings were used to develop actionable business insights and recommendations aimed at improving profitability, addressing loss-making areas, and supporting data-driven decision-making.
