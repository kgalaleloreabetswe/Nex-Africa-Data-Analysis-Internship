# Week 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis & Business Insights

## Overview

Week 2 focused on exploring the cleaned dataset from Week 1 to identify patterns, trends, relationships, and meaningful business insights.

The analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel and focused on overall sales and business performance through KPI calculations, sales trend analysis, regional analysis, and category analysis.

## Objectives

- Calculate key business performance indicators
- Analyse sales and profit trends over time
- Analyse sales, profit, and orders across regions
- Compare sales, profit, and quantity across product categories
- Identify patterns and trends in business performance
- Create meaningful charts and visualizations
- Answer relevant analytical and business questions
- Identify and document data-driven business insights

## Files

| **File** | **Description** |
| -------- | --------------- |
| `Cleaned_Data` | Cleaned and analysis-ready dataset prepared during Week 1 |
| `Analysis` | Excel workbook containing KPI calculations, analysis, visualizations, and findings |
| `Week_2_Document` | Documentation of the analysis, methodology, visualizations, findings, and business insights |

## Analysis Performed

- Core KPI analysis
- Sales analysis by year and month
- Sales and profit trend analysis
- Regional sales and profitability analysis
- Category sales, profit, and quantity analysis
- Business performance visualization

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Formulas
- Data Visualization
- Exploratory Data Analysis

## Key Outcome

The cleaned dataset from Week 1 was analysed to evaluate overall business performance and identify important sales and profitability trends.

The analysis produced key performance indicators, sales trend analysis, regional and category comparisons, visualizations, and data-driven business insights that can support informed business decision-making.
